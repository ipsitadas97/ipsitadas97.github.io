Doctoral Candidate in Economics at IGIDR, Mumbai

I am a Doctoral Candidate in Economics at the Indira Gandhi Institute of Development Research (IGIDR), Mumbai, affiliated with the Reserve Bank of India. I hold a master's degree in economics from IGIDR and a bachelor's degree from Scottish Church College, University of Calcutta.

My research focuses on corporate finance, banking, and applied microeconomics, with additional interests in macro-finance and monetary policy. Passionate about advancing knowledge in these areas, I strive to contribute meaningfully to academic and policy discussions.
